# Code of Conduct

Relay Runner's community should be useful, safe, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, design, or decisions.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar project conventions without condescension.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Protect private data. Do not repost transcripts, credentials, personal paths, or identifying screenshots.
- Respect a maintainer's request to stop disruptive, off-topic, or unsafe behavior.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), using the subject “Code of Conduct” and omitting unrelated private material. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed or handed to another reviewer when one is available.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, review threads, and project-related communication where someone is representing Relay Runner.
