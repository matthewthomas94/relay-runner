## Description

Background: RR-448 (done) scoped porting the Python services in `services/` to Rust. Read its `## Spike report` first; it holds the inventory, interface list, evidence and staged plan. Use `scripts/relay-ticket-history show RR-448` if the ticket has been archived.

RR-448 recommended a go/no-go gate after the hook and speech stages, before committing to porting the voice bridge and the daemon. Produce the evidence for that decision in `docs/investigations/RR-448-rust-port-gate.md`.

Measure, on a build with the Rust hook and Rust speech service as defaults, against the last all-Python release:

- Download size of `RelayRunner.dmg` and installed size including Application Support.
- First-run setup time and what it downloads.
- Resident memory and idle CPU of the bridge, the speech service and the daemon over 10 minutes with a session active and no speech.
- Hook latency and time to first audio.
- Defects found in the two Rust stages after release, from tickets and support diagnostics, and how many used the rollback switch.
- The size of the remaining Python: modules, lines, and third-party dependencies (expected: standard library only).

Then cost the two ways forward with the evidence gathered: continue to the bridge and daemon ports, or stop and bundle a standard-library-only Python interpreter (python-build-standalone) so users need no Python, including what that does to bundle size, signing and the hook's use of `/usr/bin/python3`.

End with one recommendation. This ticket decides nothing by itself: the user makes the call.
