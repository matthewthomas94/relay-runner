## Description

Fix the remaining lifecycle regression in RR-184's fresh-intro workspace picker. The large intro panel now finishes dismissing before `NSOpenPanel` opens, but `OnboardingController.pickFreshWorkspace` leaves `onboardingNotchOverrideActive` enabled. `AppState.setupNotchPresentation` therefore renders the small `Getting started` notch over the macOS folder chooser.

When fresh-intro Browse is pressed, clear the onboarding notch override only after the intro panel dismissal completes and before the shared workspace-picker continuation constructs or runs `NSOpenPanel`. Preserve the existing cancellation behavior: cancel must restore the final workspace prompt and reactivate its notch exactly when that prompt returns. A successful selection must remain fully retracted through the Settings handoff, persist once, and must not briefly reactivate or redundantly toggle the onboarding notch.

Keep RR-184's shared Browse ordering for Settings-hosted onboarding and General Settings unchanged. Do not change workspace copy, permission flow, provider setup, or the user's real onboarding flags.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fresh-intro Browse finishes dismissing the large intro, then records `onboardingNotchOverrideActive = false`, then invokes the picker continuation; neither onboarding notch surface is visible over `NSOpenPanel`.
- [ ] The ordering is explicit and testable: dismissal completion precedes override clearing, which precedes picker construction/execution.
- [ ] Cancelling the chooser does not persist a path or advance onboarding, and restores the same final workspace prompt with the override re-enabled once.
- [ ] Selecting a directory persists it once and hands off without reactivating or flashing the intro notch; remove any redundant success-path override transition made obsolete by the fix.
- [ ] Existing shared external-window preparation remains correct for Settings-hosted onboarding and embedded General Settings, while standalone Settings continues to open the chooser normally.
- [ ] Focused tests assert the fresh Browse override transition and event order for both cancel and success paths.
- [ ] Codex and Claude behavior remains equivalent.
- [ ] Full `swift test` and `RELAY_SKIP_APPLICATIONS_REFRESH=1 ./scripts/build-dmg.sh` pass.
