## Description

Make Workspace opening and closing feel immediate after the double-tap Shift gesture is recognized.

Instrument the full path from the second Shift release through gesture polling, project-route resolution, panel and SwiftUI construction, data loading, reveal animation, and first interactive content. Instrument closing through the point where the panel is removed from the screen.

The current reveal serializes approximately 0.24 seconds of width expansion, 0.34 seconds of height expansion, and 0.38 seconds of content reveal. Dismissal can similarly serialize about 0.22 seconds of content hiding, 0.24 seconds of width contraction, and 0.22 seconds of compacting. Replace that long chained feel with a shorter, overlapping transition. Reuse warm Workspace UI and cached board content where safe, and move route refresh or repository loading out of the first-visible-frame path.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Debug instrumentation measures gesture-recognized → first visible frame, gesture-recognized → interactive content, and close-recognized → panel hidden.
- [ ] A warm Workspace produces visible feedback within 100 ms of double-Shift recognition.
- [ ] A warm Workspace is fully visible and interactive within 300 ms of double-Shift recognition on the reference development Mac.
- [ ] Closing removes the Workspace from view within 250 ms of double-Shift recognition.
- [ ] Cold or uncached board loading shows the Workspace shell within 100 ms and refreshes content asynchronously instead of delaying the first frame.
- [ ] Reveal and dismiss phases overlap or are consolidated so their durations are not added into the current long serial chain.
- [ ] Repeated open and close cycles reuse the panel, hosting hierarchy, and valid cached snapshot where practical without displaying another project’s stale data.
- [ ] The Shift gesture remains a clean double-tap and does not fire on single Shift presses or modified key combinations.
- [ ] Work, Terminal, and Settings tabs open with the same responsive shell behavior.
- [ ] Single-project and multi-project Workspace routes both meet the first-visible target and retain correct project scope.
- [ ] Reduce Motion opens and closes without unnecessary animation delay.
- [ ] No regression introduces flicker, lost focus, duplicate panels, blocked keyboard input, or refresh work continuing after close.
- [ ] Automated performance and state tests cover warm open, cold open, close, rapid toggle reversal, cached-scope validation, and tab restoration.
