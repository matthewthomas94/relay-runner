## Description

Fix a live installed-app regression where Relay Runner keeps queued voice prompts behind a provider-turn safe-boundary after the foreground provider has already completed. Sanitized evidence from one Codex session showed a conversational command received and queued immediately, the foreground task completing about ten seconds later, and terminal delivery continuing to emit `safe_boundary_wait` with `deferral_reason: provider_turn` for roughly another 2 minutes 19 seconds. The command was not claimed until the wait had lasted about 148 seconds. Once the boundary finally cleared, the pending command and later queued turns drained serially without any implementation worker having been active.

The lifecycle records disagreed during the delay: the Relay-correlated turn had reached a terminal stale state, while a separate `origin: manual` provider-turn record remained active until immediately before delivery resumed. Diagnose whether that record is created by duplicate prompt-submit correlation, startup/session identity drift, or failure to close the correct manual record. Establish one authoritative identity and terminal transition for the actual foreground turn so an unrelated or orphaned record cannot make an idle terminal look busy indefinitely.

Treat this as a live regression of RR-238 and RR-272, especially RR-272's requirement that an idle provider with no real draft claim a queued command within 500 ms p95. Preserve the safety boundary for genuine typed or pasted terminal input. Do not inject voice into a real active manual turn, overwrite a draft, weaken newest-intent reply suppression, drop ordered inbox items, or allow a stale conversational turn to act on a newer unclaimed command.

- [x] Genuine typed and pasted drafts remain untouched, and a real manually submitted provider turn continues to block voice injection until its exact completion or an explicitly supported atomic steering handoff.
