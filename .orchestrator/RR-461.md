## Description

On 2026-11-03 the user sent a crop of the menu bar while Figma was frontmost: the notch status capsule had grown leftwards far enough to cover Figma's "Widgets" menu item and the start of the next one. The capsule is meant to be a glanceable receipt beside the notch, not a sentence that hides the frontmost app's menus. The user's ask: make this status more concise.

Why it grows that wide today (`Sources/relay-runner/Overlay/NotchStatusController.swift`):

- While the session is working, the visible label is the foreground working-progress text that the state machine stores from bridge progress traces (`StateMachine.workingProgress`, fresh for 12 s). `NotchActivityLabelPlanner.label(forWorkingProgress:)` passes it through almost verbatim, up to 180 characters.
- `NotchStatusPlacementPlanner.maximumWorkingProgressLabelWidth` is 325 pt (half of `maximumActivityLabelWidth`, 650 pt) and `maximumActivityLabelWidth` applies to every other label. At the 12 pt semibold notch font that is roughly 50 to 100 characters of text extending left from the notch, which on a 14-inch display reaches past the middle of most apps' menu bars.
- The worker-run labels already have the right idea: `label(forWorkerActivity:)` maps free text to a fixed two-word vocabulary ("Running tests", "Editing files", "Reading code"). The foreground working label skips that mapping.

Required behaviour:

1. **Resting label vocabulary.** The visible (non-hovered) working label is a short phrase from a fixed vocabulary, derived from the progress text by the same kind of mapping as `label(forWorkerActivity:)` (extend that vocabulary for foreground activity, for example "Writing ticket", "Dispatching worker", "Reading code", "Checking status", "Working"). Never show the raw progress sentence at rest. Keep the existing allowlisted state labels ("Listening", "Thinking", "Response ready", and so on) as they are.
2. **Width budget.** Lower the visible label cap so the capsule's total width stays within the notch width plus a small margin on each side: target a visible label of at most 160 pt (about 24 characters), and apply the cap to every label that can show at rest, including the "Remote: <device>" and "Listening · <name>" / "Playing · <name>" forms (truncate the name with an ellipsis rather than the state word). Keep the trailing edge anchored as today.
3. **Full text on hover only.** The existing hover reveal keeps showing the full working-progress text and the detailed run label ("RR-452 run 292: Building project - <title> (Claude)") up to the current 650 pt cap, since the user asked for that detail by hovering. Hover behaviour otherwise unchanged, including the stable static rendering covered by `testWorkingProgressHoverUsesStableStaticLabelRendering`.
4. Motion rules from `docs/` and the RelayMotion kit still apply: label swaps keep the blur-and-fade transition, no resize jitter when the mapped phrase changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `NotchStatusPlacementTests`: a 180-character working-progress text yields a resting label from the fixed vocabulary of at most 24 characters and a placement whose visible label width is at most 160 pt; hovering yields the full text within 650 pt.
- [ ] Every label in `NotchVisualLabelAllowlist` and every form built from a device or voice-target name measures at most 160 pt at the notch font, with the name truncated rather than the state word (unit test iterating the allowlist with a 40-character name).
