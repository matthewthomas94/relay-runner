## Description

Fix the active-session failure in which an internal provider continuation after a tool result is classified as a new manual terminal turn. In the observed Codex session, the original Relay-owned provider turn remained the only user-submitted turn, but an unmatched `UserPromptSubmit` arrived immediately after a `custom_tool_call_output`. The completion hook orphaned the real Relay record and created a phantom manual owner. The later provider completion could no longer correlate to the Relay turn, so its authoritative final was not delivered through TTS and two newer voice commands remained queued behind `safe_boundary_wait` without Messenger acknowledgements.

Make app-owned manual-turn ownership depend on exact evidence from Relay Runner's terminal submission boundary rather than provider hook shape alone. Provider-internal tool/result continuations must remain attached to the active physical Relay turn even when a provider version omits or changes a parent-tool marker. Preserve genuine typed or pasted terminal drafts and manual submissions. Do not use a blind timeout, globally clear provider ownership, guess from arbitrary text, restart the provider, or weaken exact-session completion matching.
