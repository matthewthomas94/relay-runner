## Description

Fix the active-session failure in which an internal provider continuation after a tool result is classified as a new manual terminal turn. In the observed Codex session, the original Relay-owned provider turn remained the only user-submitted turn, but an unmatched `UserPromptSubmit` arrived immediately after a `custom_tool_call_output`. The completion hook orphaned the real Relay record and created a phantom manual owner. The later provider completion could no longer correlate to the Relay turn, so its authoritative final was not delivered through TTS and two newer voice commands remained queued behind `safe_boundary_wait` without Messenger acknowledgements.

Make app-owned manual-turn ownership depend on exact evidence from Relay Runner's terminal submission boundary rather than provider hook shape alone. Provider-internal tool/result continuations must remain attached to the active physical Relay turn even when a provider version omits or changes a parent-tool marker. Preserve genuine typed or pasted terminal drafts and manual submissions. Do not use a blind timeout, globally clear provider ownership, guess from arbitrary text, restart the provider, or weaken exact-session completion matching.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A deterministic regression reproduces an active Relay-owned turn followed by a provider-internal custom-tool continuation whose prompt-submit hook has different native identifiers and no parent-tool marker; it must not orphan the Relay record or create a manual owner.
- [ ] In app-owned sessions, an unmatched provider `UserPromptSubmit` can create manual ownership only when corroborated by an exact Relay Runner terminal manual-submit boundary or equivalent app-owned nonce tied to that physical submission.
- [ ] A genuine typed or pasted draft and genuine manual provider submission still block voice injection until the exact protected turn terminalizes; cross-session, stale, duplicate, and ambiguous evidence remains fail-closed.
- [ ] The exact provider completion terminalizes the Relay-owned turn once, emits the authoritative final once, and makes the visible response and TTS eligible from the same response identity.
- [ ] After that terminal event, the oldest eligible queued commands are claimed and submitted in order within 500 milliseconds p95 without another message, app relaunch, provider restart, or session reset.
- [ ] Messenger handoff and acknowledgement behavior cannot silently suppress every response while foreground ownership is stranded; queued, claimed, completed, and failed states remain truthful.
- [ ] Privacy-safe diagnostics distinguish a real manual submit from an ignored or quarantined provider continuation and record the evidence source, provider/session/turn identity, decision, and drain result without prompt, transcript, reply, terminal text, secrets, or screenshots.
- [ ] Codex and Claude pass equivalent automated lifecycle, manual-boundary, completion, ordered-drain, cancellation, replacement, and TTS exact-once coverage. Any intentional hook-payload difference is documented and tested at the adapter boundary.
- [ ] A mounted signed-app smoke runs a tool-using Relay voice turn followed by at least two queued voice turns, verifies no phantom manual provider record remains, and confirms the first final plus later acknowledgements/replies are both visible and human-audible. Provider-specific authenticated gates must be reported rather than inferred.

## Live regression evidence

- The Relay-owned submit began first and remained active through a provider custom-tool call and its output.
- Roughly one second after that tool output, provider-turn state changed the Relay record to `orphaned` with reason `superseded_by_prompt_submit` and created a new `origin=manual` active record despite no Swift terminal manual-submission event and no new provider user message.
- The provider later emitted its normal task-complete event, but the Relay-owned record never reached its authoritative final path. The claimed voice sequence remained older than the durable inbox, and two newer commands stayed queued behind the phantom provider-turn boundary.
- The installed completion-hook source matched the repository source, so this is current behavior rather than a stale bundled script.
