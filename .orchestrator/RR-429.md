## Description

Spec: `docs/specs/relay-remote.md` (Relay Remote: iOS voice over SSH), committed as `2a64cc0d` on branch `claude/ios-relay-ssh-tcp-spec-ddb8f2`. The spec is not on `main` yet; if the file is missing from your worktree, read it with `git show 2a64cc0d:docs/specs/relay-remote.md`. Read these sections first: iOS app, Wire protocol, Provider parity.

Wire the speech engines chosen in RR-421 into the iOS app from RR-428, so a spoken request on the phone becomes a spoken reply on the phone while the Mac stays silent.

- STT: on-device recognition with end-of-utterance detection; each final utterance becomes one `utterance` frame with a fresh `client_id` and `captured_at`.
- TTS: speak `speech` frames in `seq` order; send `ack` with `played: false` on arrival and `played: true` after playback completes. Honour `speech_control` stop and skip.
- Audio session: configure for capture and playback together; handle interruptions (calls, Siri) and route changes; enable the background audio mode only while recording or playing and drop it otherwise.
- Input modes: push-to-talk and hands-free (continuous listening with end-of-utterance).
- Controls: stop, replay and cancel buttons send `control` frames.
- The Mac routes speech to the phone through the remote sink from RR-424; this ticket makes no Mac-side changes.

Out of scope: Mac-side changes; the user guide (RR-430).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a physical iPhone a spoken request produces a spoken reply on the phone while the Mac stays silent.
- [ ] `speech` frames play in `seq` order and `played: true` acks are sent only after playback completes (fake-server test).
- [ ] Interruption, lock screen and reconnect replay behave per the spec's Wire protocol section: no duplicated or dropped speech after a reconnect.
- [ ] Background audio mode is active only while recording or playing.
- [ ] Both input modes work and each control button sends its `control` frame.
