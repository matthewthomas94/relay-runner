## Description

RR-209 improved Workspace open/close responsiveness by reducing work before presentation, reusing warm UI, and adding latency instrumentation, but it also shortened the reveal and dismiss animations themselves. The resulting motion feels rushed. The intended behavior is immediate acknowledgement of the command followed by the deliberate Workspace animation—not a faster animation used to compensate for delayed command handling.

Preserve RR-209's useful latency, caching, and lifecycle improvements while restoring the intended reveal/dismiss pacing and choreography. Measure responsiveness as the delay from a recognized keypad gesture or notch click to the first visible animation frame. Do not use total animation completion time as the reason to compress the motion. Opening and closing must start promptly from both entry points, then run at the designed speed with equivalent Reduce Motion behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A recognized keypad Workspace command produces its first visible reveal or dismiss motion within 100 ms on the reference development Mac.
- [ ] Clicking the Workspace control in the notch view meets the same command-to-first-motion target.
- [ ] Reveal and dismiss animation durations and easing are restored to the deliberate pre-RR-209 visual pacing, or to visually equivalent documented values; they are not shortened to satisfy an end-to-end completion budget.
- [ ] Performance instrumentation reports command-to-first-motion separately from animation duration and command-to-interactive/hidden completion.
- [ ] Tests no longer conflate responsiveness with faster animation by requiring full reveal or dismissal within RR-209's compressed `300 ms`/`250 ms` budgets.
- [ ] Warm panel reuse, cached board content, asynchronous cold loading, correct project scope, tab restoration, and first-visible-shell behavior from RR-209 remain intact.
- [ ] Opening and closing from keypad and notch entry points use the same transition path and do not introduce a pre-animation pause, flicker, duplicate panel, or lost focus.
- [ ] Rapid toggle reversal remains safe and does not let a stale dismiss completion hide a reopened Workspace.
- [ ] Reduce Motion responds immediately and skips unnecessary motion without changing Workspace state outcomes.
- [ ] Focused timing/state tests cover both entry points, first-motion latency, preserved animation pacing, rapid reversal, and warm/cold presentation.
