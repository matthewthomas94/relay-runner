## Description

The user wants a brief summary of an agent's reply when they are outside the app, without a cloud push service (`docs/design/relay-remote-ios/README.md`, section "Reply notifications"; images `11-permission-notifications.png`, `28-notification-lock-screen.jpg`; Figma [2139:39787](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-39787), [2139:49523](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-49523)). The decision: local notifications only, posted by the app itself while it stays alive in the background. No APNs.

**Today:** the app is foreground-first. `AppModel.scenePhaseChanged` in `ios/RelayRemote/AppModel.swift` closes the channel to the Mac in the background unless audio is active; the audio session is active only while recording or playing (`ios/RelayRemote/Voice/AudioSessionController.swift`); `UIBackgroundModes` already declares `audio`.

**Wanted, when the reply-notifications preference is on and iOS notification permission is granted:**

- Going to the background keeps the audio session active and the connection to the Mac open, so replies keep arriving. Use the least intrusive audio-session configuration that reliably keeps the app running, and document the choice and its cost in `ios/README.md`.
- Each agent reply that arrives while the app is not in the foreground posts one local notification: the title is the agent's name, the body is the reply's spoken text (the `speech` frames for that reply, joined, trimmed to a sensible length). One notification per reply, not one per speech frame. Replies are grouped by agent (thread identifier per agent).
- Whether the reply is also spoken aloud in the background follows the existing Remote mode and Replies settings; this ticket does not change that.
- Tapping a notification opens the app on the Agents tab with that agent selected.
- If the connection to the Mac drops in the background and does not recover after the normal reconnect attempts, post one notification: "Lost connection to your Mac. Open to reconnect." Then release the audio session so the app stops holding the microphone indicator. Do not post it again until the app has been opened.
- Returning to the foreground clears delivered reply notifications for the agent whose chat is shown.

**When the preference is off, or permission is not granted:** the app behaves exactly as it does today in the background.

**Accepted trade-offs (do not try to solve them):** nothing arrives if the app is swiped away, killed, or after a reboot; the orange microphone indicator may show while the audio session is held; battery use is higher.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the preference on, backgrounding the app keeps the channel open (shown in the simulator by a reply sent from a scripted or real Mac two minutes after backgrounding producing a notification).
- [ ] The notification's title is the agent's name and its body is that reply's spoken text; a reply made of several speech frames produces one notification.
- [ ] Tapping the notification opens the Agents tab on that agent's chat, from both a backgrounded and a terminated-then-relaunched app.
- [ ] A lost connection posts the "Lost connection" notification once and releases the audio session.
- [ ] With the preference off, the background behaviour and audio-session lifetime are the same as before this ticket (existing tests unchanged and passing).
- [ ] No notification is posted for a reply that arrives while the app is in the foreground.
- [ ] `ios/README.md` and `docs/specs/relay-remote.md` describe the background behaviour, the trade-offs, and that no push service is involved.
- [ ] The app builds and all tests pass; reply-to-notification assembly, the once-only lost-connection rule and the lifecycle decision (keep alive or not) have unit tests with the notification centre and audio session faked.
