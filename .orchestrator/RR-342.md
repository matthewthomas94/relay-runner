## Description

Fix the recurrence in which a voice command is durably queued but is effectively dropped from the foreground experience because it never reaches claim and prompt submission. The live command remained at `queued` while delivery repeatedly reported `terminal_draft`, then `manual_submission` / `safe_boundary_wait`; no claim or provider acknowledgement followed during the observed interval.

Preserve genuine typed or pasted drafts and genuine manual provider work. The fix must make each barrier transition explicit and guarantee prompt drain immediately after its exact safe boundary, including when native provider completion identity changes or continuity recovery participates. Do not use a blind timeout, discard a draft, globally clear provider ownership, or inject voice text into an active manual turn.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A deterministic regression reproduces a durable voice item waiting across terminal-draft growth, manual submission, an active manual provider record, and its exact terminal completion without losing command or intent identity.
- [ ] While a genuine terminal draft or manual provider turn is active, the voice item remains safely queued and cannot overwrite, interleave with, or submit the user's typed content.
- [ ] When the draft clears or the exact manual turn terminalizes, the oldest eligible queued voice item is claimed and submitted within 500 milliseconds p95 without requiring another user message, provider restart, app relaunch, or session reset.
- [ ] Provider completion identity reconciliation and continuity recovery cannot strand the queued item behind a phantom `terminal_draft`, `manual_submission`, or provider-turn barrier after the protected work has ended.
- [ ] Privacy-safe diagnostics identify the current barrier owner, state transition, age, release reason, command and intent identity, and next drain decision without terminal text, transcript content, prompts, replies, secrets, or screenshots.
- [ ] UI and speech lifecycle feedback distinguish safely queued work from a claimed turn and from an actually failed delivery, so the user is not told or shown that a message was handled before foreground ownership exists.
- [ ] Cancellation, replacement, durable ordering, duplicate Stop events, stale completions, cross-session isolation, and restart recovery remain exact-once and newest-command safe.
- [ ] Codex and Claude pass equivalent automated barrier/drain coverage, followed by a mounted rapid-turn smoke that records queue, barrier, release, claim, acknowledgement, and final delivery timestamps.
