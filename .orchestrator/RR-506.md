## Description

Follow-up from the RR-501 spike (run 380); read its findings in RR-501 before starting.

Add a scanner that lists leftover Relay processes and other leftovers without stopping anything, exposed at `GET /v1/maintenance/leftovers`. Each result gives its reason, sorted into Tier 1 (verified Relay leftovers) and Tier 2 (path-only matches). Follow the tiers and the never-kill list in the RR-501 findings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The endpoint returns leftovers with a tier and a reason for each, and stops nothing.
- [ ] Unit tests on synthetic process tables cover: rr-48 vs rr-485 prefix, a reused workspace, a process with a TTY, unreadable argv, and a live run vetoing a match.
- [ ] The live daemon, the active session's provider and bridge, and running workers are never listed.
