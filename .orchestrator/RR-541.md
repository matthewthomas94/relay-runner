## Description

The user's rule: while they are talking, an orange dot field glows beneath all of the UI; while a reply plays, a purple one does. This applies everywhere in the app, not only in the tutorial (`docs/design/relay-remote-ios/README.md`, section "State glows"; images `22-agents-holding-to-talk.jpg`, `23-agents-playing-back.jpg`, `17-tutorial-release-to-send.png`, `19-tutorial-stop-playback.png`; Figma [2139:44337](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-44337), [2139:46237](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-46237), [2139:39989](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-39989), [2139:41749](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-41749)).

RR-533 built the dot-field view (a port of the desktop's `buildDotGrid`) with a listening style and a playback style. This ticket mounts it once at the app root, behind every screen, and drives it from voice state.

- **Listening:** from the moment recording starts (Hold or Toggle mode) until it ends, the orange field covers the bottom 32% of the screen.
- **Playback:** while any reply is playing on the phone, the purple field covers the bottom 44%.
- The field sits behind all UI: the tab bar, the dock, the text box, the agent tabs, the chat feed, and the onboarding and tutorial screens. Glass surfaces blur it as they do in the reference images. Nothing draws an opaque background over it; fix any view that does.
- It fades in and out (about 0.3 s) rather than popping. If listening starts while a reply plays, listening wins.
- With Reduce Motion on, the field still appears but does not animate.
- It shows on every tab and screen where talking or playback can happen, including Workspace and Settings while a reply plays.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Holding to talk on the Agents tab shows the orange field across the bottom 32%, visible behind the dock and tab bar, and it fades out on release.
- [ ] Playing a reply shows the purple field across the bottom 44%, and it fades out when playback ends or is stopped.
- [ ] Switching to Workspace or Settings during playback keeps the purple field visible behind that tab.
- [ ] The field is one view at the app root, driven by a single observable voice state; there is no per-screen copy.
- [ ] Simulator screenshots of the listening and playing states match `22-agents-holding-to-talk.jpg` and `23-agents-playing-back.jpg` for height, colour and layering.
- [ ] The app builds and all tests pass; the state-to-glow mapping (idle, listening, playing, both) has a unit test.
