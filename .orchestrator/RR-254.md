## Description

Fix the Program Workspace regression where the first visible ticket in the
Backlog lane does not register hover events, while tickets below it respond
normally. This is a focused follow-up to RR-249: the general stale-hover and
grab-cursor lifecycle fix is merged, but the topmost Backlog card still has a
position-specific interaction hole.

Reproduce the failure through the mounted `ProgramWorkColumnPanel` and
`ProgramColumnTicketScrollView` hierarchy. The first card is structurally
different from later cards: it follows `ProgramDropIndicator`, receives the
special first-card `scrollBoundary.topInset`, and sits at the leading edge of
the clipped AppKit scroll viewport. Determine whether an invisible sibling or
overlay intercepts the hit, the drop-indicator and card frames overlap, the
tracking view does not cover the card's full visible bounds, or pointer
containment is reconciled against stale coordinates. Treat these as hypotheses,
not a prescribed root cause.

Make the smallest geometry or event-routing correction that gives the first
card the same hover, cursor, click, and drag behavior as every later card. Do
not remove the leading drop target or first-card scroll boundary merely to make
the test pass. Preserve RR-249's single-card hover ownership and cursor
lifecycle, the RR-228 top scroll endpoint, lane drop feedback, click-to-open,
drag thresholds, auto-scroll, and ordinary vertical scrolling.
