## Description

Build an end-to-end verification matrix for accepted voice turns after the lifecycle broker lands. Exercise process restarts, duplicate callbacks, delayed acknowledgements, recovery, cancellation, and competing outputs, with mounted app coverage for both providers. Bind this ticket to the accepted lifecycle-broker ticket before promotion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A deterministic fault-injection harness covers messenger, foreground provider, daemon, bridge, and Swift restarts at each lifecycle boundary.
- [ ] Each accepted turn is proven to have exactly one claim, one terminal acknowledgement, and at most one user-visible effect.
- [ ] Cancellation and replacement tests prove that revoked turns cannot emit a late effect.
- [ ] Mounted Codex and Claude runs record correlated identifiers for accepted playback without duplicate effects.
- [ ] Normal-path acknowledgement-to-playback latency is measured and remains at or below 500 milliseconds at p95.
- [ ] Failures produce bounded allowlisted diagnostics that identify the violated lifecycle invariant.
