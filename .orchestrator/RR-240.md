## Description

Reduce the notch's visible status vocabulary to a small, intentional allowlist.
Only these states may expand the notch and display status text:

- runtime STT states: **Listening**, **Sending voice**, and
  **Recording cancelled**;
- runtime TTS states: **Response ready**, **Preparing speech**, **Playing**,
  and **Response cancelled**;
- active bridge startup: **Starting up...**, including the existing
  **Starting session** hover wording;
- foreground command receipt: **Acknowledged**; and
- ordinary Relay Actions or Relay Vision activity: **Using screen**.

Preserve the current orange listening and blue response/playback glyph
treatments. `Thinking` is not part of this allowlist: it is a neutral working
state and should become glyph-only. Likewise, **Starting up...** means active
bridge startup, not the onboarding **Getting started** label. Keep
**Using screen** for ordinary ActionGlow activity; do not retain or re-enable
the legacy **Awaiting approval** path, because current Relay control does not
use `propose_action`.

Every other activity may continue to drive the animated working glyph, but it
must not widen the notch, start the label carousel, or reveal text on hover.
This includes reconnect/session readiness, program and service status,
Workspace updates, dependencies, worker lifecycle and activity, foreground
reasoning/progress traces, board changes, onboarding, and permission setup.
Idle still uses the static neutral glyph.

Suppress presentation, not state. Keep worker/run tracking, public
orchestration traces, messenger context, board state, diagnostics,
accessibility semantics, and permission/onboarding flows available to their
other consumers. This ticket intentionally supersedes the broad background
status expansion introduced by RR-94, RR-112, RR-120, RR-125, and RR-145, and
the hover-detail behavior preserved by RR-126, RR-139, and RR-143. Update those
expectations rather than treating the narrower product decision as a
regression.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Introduce one testable visual-label allowlist for the notch rather than
      scattering exclusions across individual call sites.
- [ ] The retained STT labels are exactly **Listening**, **Sending voice**, and
      **Recording cancelled**, with the current orange listening glyph and
      recording-state behavior unchanged.
- [ ] The retained TTS labels are exactly **Response ready**,
      **Preparing speech**, **Playing**, and **Response cancelled**, with the
      current blue response/playback glyph and response-preview behavior
      unchanged.
- [ ] **Starting up...** remains visible during active bridge startup and
      **Starting session** remains its hover detail. **Acknowledged** and
      ordinary **Using screen** activity remain visible under their existing
      timing rules.
- [ ] `Thinking`, `Reconnecting session`, `Waiting session`,
      `Session ready`, `Checking status`, `Checking for updates`,
      `Waiting dependency`, worker status/activity copy, setup and permission
      labels, dynamic foreground progress, and board/lifecycle trace text do
      not expand the notch or appear in its visible label carousel.
- [ ] A suppressed status by itself cannot cause initial label reveal, carousel
      rotation, hover expansion, scrolling text, or residual width from the
      preceding allowed label.
- [ ] Suppressed active work still selects the animated neutral working glyph;
      the glyph does not become static merely because its text is hidden.
      Truly idle state remains static.
- [ ] Transitioning from an allowed label to suppressed work retracts cleanly
      to glyph-only presentation, and transitioning back expands once without
      stale copy, geometry jumps, or hover oscillation.
- [ ] Foreground and worker traces continue reaching the messenger,
      orchestrator lifecycle, Workspace, diagnostics, and any non-notch
      consumers. The change does not delete or stop publishing those events.
- [ ] Onboarding and permission flows remain completable after their notch
      labels are suppressed; native prompts, companion guidance, controls, and
      accessibility descriptions remain available elsewhere.
- [ ] Ordinary ActionGlow retains **Using screen**. The implementation does not
      restore `propose_action` or depend on **Awaiting approval** to satisfy the
      retained screen-use requirement.
- [ ] Codex and Claude produce identical glyph-only behavior for suppressed
      progress and identical visible behavior for allowed states.
- [ ] Focused tests cover every `OverlayState`, bridge startup and recovery,
      Workspace loading, active and stalled runs, dependency waits, dynamic
      traces, onboarding/permissions, allowlisted transitions, and hover
      behavior.
- [ ] Relevant notch placement, state-machine, bridge recovery, Workspace,
      permission setup, and provider-neutral status tests pass.
