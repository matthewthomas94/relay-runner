## Description

Relay Runner has an experimental custom voices feature: the user records or imports a voice sample, a separate cloning runtime (KokoClone/Kanade) is installed under `~/Library/Application Support/relay-runner/services/custom-voice/`, and the TTS worker renders replies with that voice when `[tts] custom_voice_id` is set. On 2026-11-06 the user decided to remove it entirely: it does not work the way they want, it makes no meaningful difference to the app, and they will not get around to improving it. Remove the whole feature, not just the Settings section, so no dead code remains. The built-in Kokoro voices, the voice preview, the Mac/phone audio lease and the phone audio path (`remote_audio.py`, `speech_audio` frames) stay exactly as they are.
