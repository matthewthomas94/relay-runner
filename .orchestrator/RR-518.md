## Description

In a voice turn, the foreground PM can send a reply through `"$RELAY_REPLY_HELPER" reply` (`rust/relay-rs/src/reply.rs`). That reply is accepted as the command's one authoritative final (`outcome:<seq>:<id>`). Any later helper reply for the same command, and the Stop-hook completion final, are then deduplicated against it.

So when the PM sends a short "on it" or "reading it now" through the helper and the real answer afterwards, only the short one is spoken. It happened on 2026-11-16. For command 394 the user dropped a diagnostic report into Downloads. The PM sent "Got it, reading the report now" through the helper, about eight seconds after the command. That was played as the final (`/tmp/relay_speech_events.jsonl`: `outcome:394:...` proposed, accepted, played at 1794816320–322). The findings sent through the helper later in the same turn were never spoken. The user asked why the report summary didn't come back. Earlier turns that day (commands 389, 392 and 393) followed the same pattern and probably lost their real answers too.

Make this impossible by design, not by the PM remembering a rule:
- The spoken final for a command is the PM's **last** reply in that turn, or, if it sent none, the completion hook's final provider message. An earlier reply in the same turn is interim. Either it's spoken as an interim update (a non-authoritative `handoff` / progress kind, deduplicated against the messenger's own acknowledgement), or it's held. It never takes the final slot.
- A practical design: the helper's reply stays pending until the turn ends (Stop), or until a newer reply replaces it, and only then becomes authoritative. Alternatively, add an explicit `reply --interim` (or `progress`) mode, have the helper refuse an ack-like reply as final before Stop, and make the completion hook speak the final message when it differs from an interim reply. Pick whichever is simplest and robust. Keep the guarantees that a final is spoken once, is never stale after a newer command, and the RR-512 delivery fixes still hold.
- Update the provider instructions (the source behind `Sources/relay-orchestrator-mcp/Instructions.generated.swift` and the voice-session system prompt text, regenerated, not hand-edited) to say what the contract now is: use the interim mode for acknowledgements, and the final comes from the last reply or the turn's end.
- Look in `rust/relay-rs/src/bridge/finals.rs` (`handle_orchestrator_reply_control`, `schedule_foreground_reply_fallback`), `messenger_runtime.rs` and `reply.rs`.
