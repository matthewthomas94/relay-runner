## Description

Follow-up from the RR-501 spike (run 380); read its findings in RR-501 before starting.

Add `RELAY_RUN_ID` and `RELAY_WORKSPACE` to the worker environment through the `start_process` overrides (`worker.rs` around lines 569-571). Processes that escape the process group but inherit the environment can then be matched by the leftover scanner.
