## Description

Fix the RR-196 regression where the `You’re all set /` screen appears briefly, collapses back to the compact `Getting started` notch, and never opens the Workspace board. The installed app reproduces this after a successful workspace selection.

The current completion hold begins as soon as `presentCompletionPrompt()` schedules a fresh `BoardRevealContainerView.animateReveal`. Normal reveal timing is approximately 0.24 seconds to full width, 0.34 seconds to expanded, and 0.38 seconds for content translation/opacity. The 0.85-second completion hold can therefore call `dismiss` before the reveal completion chain has finished. `BoardRevealSurfaceView.animate(to:)` cancels the active animation and clears its stored completion, allowing the panel to visually reach compact state without invoking `OnboardingIntroController.dismiss` completion. That leaves the onboarding notch override and first-run shell lock active, so `openWorkspaceAfterCompletion` never runs.

Make completion sequencing explicit: the presenter must notify `OnboardingController` after the completion surface has fully revealed (or immediately in Reduced Motion / already-visible cases), and only then should the visible hold begin. Dismissal must finish, clear `Getting started`, unlock the app shell, and invoke the real Workspace work-tab opener exactly once. Do not paper over the problem with a longer guessed delay or duplicate the Workspace animation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `OnboardingIntroPresenting` exposes completion-surface reveal completion, and `OnboardingIntroController` forwards the actual `BoardRevealContainerView.animateReveal` completion when a new surface is created.
- [ ] When the completion content is already on a revealed surface, or Reduced Motion bypasses animation, the reveal-complete callback still fires exactly once without an arbitrary delay.
- [ ] `OnboardingController` starts the `You’re all set /` hold only after reveal completion, never while the panel is still expanding or its content is still translating/fading.
- [ ] After the hold, onboarding dismissal completes before the notch override and first-run shell lock are cleared, then `openWorkspaceAfterCompletion` runs exactly once and opens the actual Work tab through the existing board reveal path.
- [ ] The compact `Getting started` panel cannot remain visible after successful completion; cancellation of the workspace picker continues to restore the picker without finishing onboarding.
- [ ] First launch, interrupted/resumed onboarding, manual Redo Onboarding, and Reduced Motion all preserve their existing flag and workspace behavior.
- [ ] Regression tests model an asynchronous completion reveal and prove that no hold/dismiss/open occurs before reveal completion, then prove dismissal/unlock/Workspace ordering after it.
- [ ] Focused onboarding tests and the full Swift suite pass.
