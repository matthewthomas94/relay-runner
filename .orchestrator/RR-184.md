## Description

If the intro chooser is cancelled, do not mutate configuration or complete the intro; restore the same final workspace step so Browse can be retried. Settings-hosted pickers must not mutate their draft/config on cancellation. Preserve the existing empty-string home-folder sentinel anywhere the UI still offers `Use Home Folder`.

Keep the RR-183 permission sequence, shared `.tts` particle renderer, readable cinematic timing, permission drag companion, and post-onboarding Settings permission management unchanged.
