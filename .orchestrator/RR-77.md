## Description

The user wants clicking the Relay Runner menu bar app icon at the top of the
screen to bring up Settings. Today the app uses `MenuBarExtra`, whose icon click
opens the tray menu containing actions such as Start/End Session, Record, Show
Board, Settings, updates, and quit.

Refine the interaction so an icon click opens or focuses Relay Runner Settings,
while preserving a clear way to reach the rest of the tray actions. If SwiftUI
`MenuBarExtra` cannot express the desired primary-click behavior safely, evaluate
a narrow AppKit `NSStatusItem` bridge rather than broad menu-bar rewrites.

Likely areas to inspect:

- `Sources/relay-runner/App/RelayRunnerApp.swift`
- `Sources/relay-runner/MenuBar/MenuBarView.swift`
- `Sources/relay-runner/Settings/SettingsWindow.swift`
