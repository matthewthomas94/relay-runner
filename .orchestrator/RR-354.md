## Description

Artifact-backed projects currently reject every branchless research spike in Daemon.dispatch with the error that the artifact lifecycle does not yet own branchless spike results. This prevents RR-269, an explicitly read-only meeting-notes architecture spike, from starting even though its Codex Astra/Max sizing is saved correctly.

Implement native spike dispatch and daemon-owned structured result persistence for artifact-backed projects. Preserve the existing read-only snapshot isolation and structured report validation. Persist ticket status, run identity, findings, and lifecycle outcomes through the canonical artifact writer, with recovery and idempotency consistent with existing artifact lifecycle behavior. Keep the artifact-backed lifecycle enabled; do not route around it by switching the project to the legacy lifecycle or treating research as implementation. Removing the dispatch guard alone is insufficient.

Inspect the existing dispatch, spike completion/recovery, artifact lifecycle coordination, and board projection paths. A spike can validly conclude go, no-go, or further evidence; these are distinct from worker execution or malformed-result failures. Preserve branchless execution, no implementation review/merge, and no automatic promotion of backlog dependents. The daemon remains the only writer of spike reports and canonical ticket state.

RR-269 is the motivating reproduction only. Do not perform its research, launch recordings or hardware benchmarks, change its acceptance criteria, or automatically dispatch it from this implementation worker. Report when the fix is integrated and what installed-daemon update, if any, is required before the foreground PM retries it. Do not rebuild/reinstall the app or switch lifecycle modes as part of this ticket.
