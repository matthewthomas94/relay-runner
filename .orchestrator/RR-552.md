## Description

Handoff for George (the user asked for this work to go to George). Build the Relay Remote UI the user refined in Figma on 2026-11-27. Source of truth: Figma file "Relay-Runner" (key `IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw`), section "Relay Remote (iOS) · Final" (node `2139:37371`): Agents screens 21–25 (`2139:44056`, `2139:44337`, `2139:46237`, `2139:48749`, `2139:48996`), Settings 27 (`2139:49386`) and the note "Dock (Agents, 21–25)" (`2159:10342`) beside "Styles & motion".

**1. Dock: the talk button moves into the text box** (`ios/RelayRemote/Agents/AgentDock.swift`)
- Remove the wide centre Talk button.
- The Send button in the text box becomes the talk button and the send button in one: white primary fill (as the old Talk button), 36 pt, radius 10, dark icon.
  - Text box empty, not talking: **mic** icon. Hold or tap to talk follows the Talk setting (Hold / Toggle), with the current gestures, haptics and live transcript.
  - Talking: **sound wave** (`audioLines`) icon; release (Hold) or tap (Toggle) sends.
  - The user has typed text: **arrow up**, tap sends the text. Clearing the text brings the mic back.
  - The icon swaps with `RelayMotion` / `.relayControlSwap`, as the controls swap now.
- The row under the text box: **Replay** and **Stop** as labelled secondary glass buttons (icon plus text label, the `.control` glass the square buttons use now), sharing the row's width equally. While talking, **Cancel** replaces Replay, as now. Disabled and dimmed states stay as they are.
- The dock stays its own glass panel above the tab bar (radius 20, 10 pt padding). The user tried joining it to the tab bar and asked to keep them separate.

**2. Settings: one panel** (`ios/RelayRemote/MainApp/SettingsTabView.swift`, `SettingsSection`)
- Instead of a glass panel per section, put every section in one glass panel like the desktop Settings detail panel: the section headings (Voice, Orchestrator, Mac, Pairing, and Testing where it shows) sit inside the panel, 24 pt between sections, rows keep their hairline dividers. Panel padding 18 top, 22 bottom, 16 sides; radius 16; same `.panel` glass. "Also unpair it on your Mac." stays under Pairing.

**3. Dock morphs into the tab bar between tabs** (`MainTabView.swift`, `GlassTabBar.swift`, `AgentsScreen.swift`)
- Leaving Agents for Workspace or Settings, the dock's glass panel melts down into the tab bar and is absorbed by it; coming back to Agents it rises back out of the tab bar into its place. It must read as gooey liquid glass: the two glass shapes merge and separate like liquid, not a fade or a slide.
- Use iOS 26 Liquid Glass morphing: put the dock and the tab bar in one `GlassEffectContainer` with a shared `@Namespace` and `glassEffectID`s, so the system blends the shapes as they meet and part. The dock's contents (text box and buttons; the agent tab strip above it is not part of the dock) fade quickly as it leaves and after it arrives, so only the glass morphs.
- Keep tab switches snappy, about 200 ms to 300 ms with `RelayMotion.move`, per the standing Relay Remote rule that frequent actions stay fast. Reduce Motion: a plain crossfade.
- While the keyboard is up the tab bar still steps aside, as now; the dock stays on the keyboard.
