## Description

Build the splash, screens 01–05 of the final design (`docs/design/relay-remote-ios/README.md`, section "Splash"; images `01-splash-r.png` to `05-splash-permissions.png`; Figma [2139:37375](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-37375), [2139:37391](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-37391), [2139:37407](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-37407), [2139:39649](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-39649), [2139:39665](https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=2139-39665)). It is a port of the desktop intro in `Sources/relay-runner/Onboarding/OnboardingIntroController.swift`. Replace the placeholder body of `SplashView` from RR-533, using its hero layout, cursor, line-swap transition and dot field.

**Sequence**

1. "R/" holds for 0.7 s.
2. "Relay Runner /" types out, one character every 0.029 s, growing from the "R".
3. The purple (playback-style) dot field fades up from the bottom over 4 s with a smoothstep curve, covering the bottom 44% of the screen.
4. The text swaps to "First thing's first /" and holds for 1.05 s.
5. The text swaps to "I need a few permissions /" and holds for 1.4 s, then the flow moves on to the permission steps.

Each swap is a 0.24 s fade with a blur to 6 pt and a scale to 96%. The " /" cursor pulses throughout. The hero text sits at the same position as on every other onboarding screen. A tap anywhere skips to the end of the splash. With Reduce Motion on, the splash is skipped entirely. Read the desktop controller for any timing not listed here and match it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On first launch the five beats play in order with the timings above (covered by a test of the sequence's schedule, not only by eye).
- [ ] Simulator screenshots at each beat match the five reference images for text, position and the dot field's height and colour.
- [ ] A tap during any beat ends the splash and moves to the permission steps.
- [ ] With Reduce Motion on, the splash does not show.
- [ ] The splash plays only as part of first-run onboarding, not on later launches.
- [ ] The app builds and all tests pass.
