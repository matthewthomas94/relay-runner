## Description

Follow-up from the RR-501 spike (run 380); read its findings in RR-501 before starting.

After `reconcile_on_startup`, and about every 15 minutes, stop the scanner's Tier 1 leftovers: SIGTERM, a grace period, then SIGKILL, only on verified targets. Log each stop and emit a lifecycle trace event. Add a config switch with three values: off, report, stop.
