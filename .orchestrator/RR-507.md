## Description

Follow-up from the RR-501 spike (run 380); read its findings in RR-501 before starting.

After `reconcile_on_startup`, and about every 15 minutes, stop the scanner's Tier 1 leftovers: SIGTERM, a grace period, then SIGKILL, only on verified targets. Log each stop and emit a lifecycle trace event. Add a config switch with three values: off, report, stop.

## Required inputs

- The default for the switch (report-only or stop), and whether Tier 2 matches may ever be stopped automatically. Both were left open by RR-501.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Only Tier 1 leftovers are stopped, and only after re-verifying identity just before signalling.
- [ ] Every stop is logged and traced; with the switch on `report` nothing is stopped.
- [ ] Tests cover the grace period and the off and report settings.
