## Description

Investigate a live regression in which a simple non-action self-explanation turn produced both a fast Messenger answer and a second PM answer. The intended product contract is that the Messenger owns these conversational answers; the foreground PM observes what was already delivered and remains silent unless it has a key missing fact, correction, or other genuinely novel information.

This behavior was previously addressed by RR-263 and the dedicated Relay Runner self-explanation routing. Determine whether the current incident is two independently authored user-facing replies, semantic-coverage arbitration failing to suppress an equivalent PM completion, a completion-hook or fallback bypass, repeated playback of one reply, or another path. Distinguish terminal-visible provider output from speech and response-pill delivery so the investigation does not conflate internal context with what the user experiences as another answer.

Trace the current provider-neutral path through self-explanation classification, Messenger response generation, PM completion, reply encoding and completion hooks, semantic coverage, speech coordination, and TTS realization. Produce an evidence-backed root cause and the smallest implementation-ready repair plan. Preserve actionable-turn lifecycle follow-ups: acknowledgements may still be followed by results, blockers, or critical questions when they add new information.
