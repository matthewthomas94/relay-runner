## Description

Correct three defects found in the post-merge audit of RR-98's first-run Workspace pull-down and typewriter intro.

First, `showFreshAutomatic()` currently writes the `.onboarding-started` sentinel and then recomputes `wasInterrupted` while deciding whether the automatic intro is eligible. That makes a genuinely pristine launch look interrupted, so the intro never presents. Capture eligibility from the pre-sentinel state, then persist the sentinel before presenting the transient panel so a termination during the intro still resumes through the established recovery flow. Manual/re-run onboarding, interrupted setup, completed setup, and Reduce Motion must continue to bypass the cinematic appropriately.

Second, the hero text currently centers against the full-screen panel even though the supplied design centers it inside the expanded Workspace surface. Derive the text layout from the reveal container's expanded frame (including its short-display clamp) so the baseline remains visually centered in the black surface on the 1728×1117 reference display and on taller or shorter displays.

Third, the supplied terminal erase sequence shows `R/` followed by `/`; the current renderer always inserts a separator space and produces `R /`. Preserve the normal phrase spacing while matching those final frames exactly.

Keep the existing RR-98 timing, pull-down animation, click/key skip behavior, Reduce Motion bypass, VoiceOver label, notch override, cleanup, and handoff to Agent Choice unchanged except where required for these corrections.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pristine automatic launch evaluates intro eligibility from state captured before `.onboarding-started` is written, presents the intro exactly once, and writes the sentinel before the transient panel is shown.
- [ ] Interrupted, recovery, completed, manual/re-run, and Reduce Motion flows bypass the cinematic and enter the same onboarding state they did after RR-98.
- [ ] An integration-level controller test proves the pristine automatic path presents the intro rather than jumping directly to Agent Choice, while interrupted and Reduce Motion paths remain covered.
- [ ] The hero is centered within the reveal container's expanded Workspace frame rather than the full display, including the 1728×1117 reference geometry, a taller display, and the short-display height clamp.
- [ ] The final erase frames match the supplied design exactly: `R/`, then `/`, with no separator space.
- [ ] Existing type/erase/hold timing, click/key cleanup, accessibility behavior, persistent `Getting started` notch status, and overlay-to-Agent-Choice handoff remain intact.
- [ ] Focused and full Swift test suites pass.
