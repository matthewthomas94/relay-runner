## Description

Upgrade the wheel opening and closing from the updated Figma keyframes: https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=1752-9156 . The latest user request replaces the circular sweep with simultaneous tile convergence and blur; the centre UI exits last.

On close, complete tiles (surface, icon and label) move inward together over 200 ms using the keyframe offsets: (0,30), (-20,20), (-22,-14), (0,-29), (20,-20), (20,20) points. Each tile retains its size while its blur rises to 48 points. The black disc stays full size through this phase. The centre remains fully visible while its blur begins 50 ms into the 350 ms exit and ramps to 24 points over the final 300 ms. During the final 150 ms the disc and blurred tiles contract to zero, the tiles fade, and the centre continues blurring and fades last. Tile blur and the centre stay clipped inside the disc. Opening reverses this 350 ms timeline: the disc and centre appear first, then tiles spread outward and sharpen together while the centre finishes sharpening over the first 300 ms. Reuse the existing wheel geometry, assets, actions, paging, accessibility and provider-neutral session model.
