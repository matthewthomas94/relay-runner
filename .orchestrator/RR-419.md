## Description

Upgrade the wheel opening and closing from the Figma storyboard: https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=1752-9156 . The updated design and latest user correction reverse the directions to clockwise exit and counterclockwise entry, with the centre UI exiting first.

On close, the stationary centre UI blurs and fades first over 90 ms. Complete segments (surface, icon and label) then blur and fade in place clockwise from the top, in slot order 0,1,2,3,4,5, over 200 ms. The black disc stays opaque and full size until these finish, then contracts around the fixed centre to zero over 150 ms. Opening reverses the 440 ms timeline: disc expansion, counterclockwise segment reveal in order 5,4,3,2,1,0, then centre reveal and sharpening. No rotation, translation or bounce. Reuse the existing wheel geometry, assets, actions, paging, accessibility and provider-neutral session model.
