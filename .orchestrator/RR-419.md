## Description

Upgrade the wheel opening and closing from the Figma storyboard: https://www.figma.com/design/IGjwpE5m7vLaCThHgoy0cw/Relay-Runner?node-id=1752-9156 . The user's direction correction is counterclockwise for exit.

On close, complete segments (surface, icon and label) blur and fade in place counterclockwise from the top, in slot order 0,5,4,3,2,1. The black disc stays opaque and full size during this 200 ms sweep. It then contracts around the fixed centre to zero over 150 ms while the stationary centre button blurs and fades. Opening plays this timeline in reverse: disc expansion and centre sharpening, followed by clockwise segment reveal in order 1,2,3,4,5,0. No rotation, translation or bounce. Reuse the existing wheel geometry, assets, actions, paging, accessibility and provider-neutral session model.
