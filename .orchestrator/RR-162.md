## Description

Fix the session-start regression introduced by the persistent messenger architecture. A new embedded session currently starts the provider with the Relay bridge bootstrap prompt, successfully creates `/tmp/voice_bridge.sock`, and then remains indefinitely at `Waiting for background terminal` because the installed Relay bridge instructions require the foreground provider turn to keep a blocking shell listener alive forever. The terminal therefore never becomes a normal interactive Codex or Claude session.

Do not fix this by merely deleting the wait loop: `/tmp/voice_cmd_ready` is currently consumed by that foreground listener, while `_should_fanout_raw_instruction_to_orchestrator` deliberately disables the daemon-side command path. Replace the blocking bootstrap contract with a session-owned, nonblocking delivery design that keeps the foreground provider authoritative and delivers every accepted voice turn to it while leaving the terminal usable immediately. Preserve typed interaction, newest-intent/preemption metadata, messenger/orchestrator authority boundaries, bridge recovery, and clean End Session behavior. Keep the implementation as small as the architecture permits and document any intentional provider-specific transport difference.

Also fix persistent messenger startup under the app/launchd environment. `/tmp/voice_bridge.log` currently reports `[messenger] provider command not found: codex` even though the foreground launcher correctly resolves `/Applications/ChatGPT.app/Contents/Resources/codex`. Reuse or introduce equivalent provider-aware executable resolution for the messenger rather than relying on launchd's restricted `PATH`. Claude must resolve its configured absolute command or known installer location with equivalent diagnostics and recovery behavior.

The generated `scripts/relay-bridge` templates are the source of truth for installed Codex and Claude skills. Update them to match the repaired lifecycle, and rebuild/install only from source artifacts. Do not patch `/Applications/Relay Runner.app` or the installed skill files directly.

- [ ] Focused tests reproduce the old startup hang and missing-provider-command failure, then verify the nonblocking startup/delivery design for Codex and Claude.
