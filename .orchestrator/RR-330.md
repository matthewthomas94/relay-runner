## Description

When the Workspace displays the “Setting up your project...” loading state, the
loader is positioned at the base of the notch-backed view instead of being
visually centered within it. Center the complete setup-loader presentation in
the notch view’s visible surface, accounting for the existing top navigation
and notch geometry rather than centering only inside the already-offset Work
content region.

Preserve the existing copy, typography and foreground treatment delivered by
RR-318, along with the current loading lifecycle, timing, animation, background,
hit testing, and transition into the populated Workspace. Do not reposition
normal board content, the Terminal or System Settings tabs, or other notch
status presentations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While “Setting up your project...” is active, the complete loader presentation is visually centered horizontally and vertically within the notch view’s visible surface, rather than sitting at its base.
- [ ] Centering accounts for the Workspace navigation/notch chrome and does not inherit the normal Work content top offset in a way that shifts the loader downward.
- [ ] RR-318’s copy, section-heading typography, foreground emphasis, and accessibility behavior remain unchanged.
- [ ] Loading state transitions, timing, animation, hit testing, refresh behavior, and transition to the populated Workspace remain unchanged.
- [ ] Normal Work board content, Terminal, System Settings, menus, session controls, and other notch status states retain their current geometry.
- [ ] Focused layout regression coverage proves the setup loader is centered against the notch view bounds rather than the offset content bounds.
- [ ] A fresh mounted app is checked during initial project setup on a notched display; the loader is visibly centered and does not jump or drift when the Workspace content appears.

## Provider parity

This Workspace layout is shared app UI and must behave identically for Codex
and Claude sessions.
