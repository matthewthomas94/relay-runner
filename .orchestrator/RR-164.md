## Description

Restore the brief visual confirmation that an integrated Relay session is actually ready. The app-owned bridge is healthy and voice commands are being claimed, but the startup presentation currently goes from `Starting up...` directly to idle/not-working when the watchdog first confirms the daemon and consumer heartbeat. That removes the expected ready pill and lower overlay, and the notch contracts without showing a readiness outcome.

On the first confirmed healthy consumer heartbeat for a newly started or recovered menu session, surface a dedicated `Session ready` state. It should use the existing working-state notch reveal, show the matching compact lower pill and purple working-state particle overlay, remain visible for two seconds, then dismiss cleanly to the normal idle session glyph. Orange remains reserved for listening, recording, and Relay Vision. Do not synthesize readiness from process launch alone, repeat it on every watchdog tick, or interfere with recording, acknowledgement, TTS, tool-action, recovery-failure, or workspace states.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A menu-started Codex or Claude session surfaces `Session ready` only after the watchdog confirms both the bridge daemon and active consumer heartbeat.
- [ ] The notch enters the working presentation, extends `Session ready` once, and contracts through the existing two-second reveal behavior without double-firing.
- [ ] The compact lower pill and purple working-state particle overlay appear for the same readiness window, then dismiss to idle; readiness never uses the orange listening/recording palette.
- [ ] Readiness is emitted once per initial session or successful recovery, not on every watchdog tick.
- [ ] A process that is merely launching, context-backed without a live consumer, failed, stopped, or recovering does not report ready.
- [ ] Recording, acknowledgement, response-ready, speaking, ActionGlow, workspace loading, and recovery-failure presentations continue to take precedence and behave unchanged.
- [ ] Codex and Claude use the same shared readiness transition and copy.
- [ ] Focused state-machine, watchdog, notch-label, overlay-theme, and auto-dismiss tests pass with the full Swift/Python/static suites.
- [ ] A Developer ID-signed installed-app smoke confirms `Starting up...` transitions to `Session ready`, the lower pill/overlay appears, and both recede after two seconds while the bridge remains healthy.
