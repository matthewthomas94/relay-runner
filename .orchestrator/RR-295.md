## Description

Replace only Relay Runner's neutral white **Working** loader with the attached V2 design. Translate the SVG geometry and animation into the native Swift notch renderer; the app must not load the SVG at runtime.

The V2 design uses the existing four 3-point white dots and rotates them counterclockwise from `0rad` to `-18.85rad` over two seconds with linear, infinite motion. Give **Working** its own motion parameters so the static **Not working** state and the orange **Listening** and blue **Playing** treatments retain their current geometry and behavior.

Package the result as a standalone Desktop demo app without replacing or launching the installed app and without regenerating release archives.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The attached SVG is treated as the authoritative design input; its SHA-256 remains `a647a415d95415dada3e7ce9e97a674d07b762859b48a2d48732c6e25fdf8866`.
- [ ] **Working** renders four 3-point white dots and rotates counterclockwise from `0rad` to `-18.85rad` over two seconds with linear, continuous motion.
- [ ] **Not working** remains static, and **Listening** and **Playing** retain their current accent colors and 0.6-second motion.
- [ ] The existing Reduce Motion policy disables the Working rotation without changing the static artwork.
- [ ] Focused Swift tests cover the Working-specific glyph mapping, geometry, duration, direction, full rotation amount, adjacent states, and Reduce Motion behavior.
- [ ] The shared renderer behaves the same in Codex and Claude sessions; no provider-specific implementation path is introduced.
- [ ] `./scripts/build-dmg.sh --app-only` produces a release app bundle without refreshing `/Applications/Relay Runner.app` or regenerating the DMG and Sparkle ZIP.
- [ ] The result is copied to a fresh Desktop `.app` path, passes `codesign --verify --deep --strict`, and has the same executable SHA-256 as the staged `dist/Relay Runner.app`.
- [ ] The installed app, existing DMG, and existing Sparkle ZIP retain their pre-build hashes and modification times.
- [ ] A mounted visual check confirms the new Working motion and unchanged Not working, Listening, Playing, and Reduce Motion states; if that physical check cannot be completed, preserve the exact verification blocker and resume step.
