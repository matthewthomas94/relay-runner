## Description

Implement the chosen native microphone/system-capture backend against the shared store. Retain separate source identity and clock placement; do not append asynchronous callback buffers in arrival order. Bounded queues and chunk files must avoid holding an entire meeting in memory. Keep realtime callbacks free of blocking DB/network work. Finalize chunks at the accepted cadence and publish their durable manifests; Stop flushes each source's tail.

Provide explicit microphone/system permission states, source meters, start/stop and a visible recording indicator. Denied system audio never silently becomes a complete dual-source session: require a deliberate microphone-only choice or fail cleanly. Model readiness belongs to ASR/coordinator, not capture. A device change starts a new epoch with a visible gap/recovery state. Test own-app/Relay TTS contamination and use the agreed exclusion/coexistence policy. [Anarlog tap](https://github.com/fastrepl/anarlog/blob/b9146a707f4da2121d3438bce38155b31e5007f1/crates/audio-actual/src/speaker/macos.rs), [Logue source timing](https://github.com/bitwize-ai/Logue/blob/ea61172f33970d40b9f9b5e4c52f30eb82836e58/Logue/Engine/AudioTimelineMixer.swift).
