## Description

The simulated Relay Runner notch/status surface currently creates hard, sharp contact corners where its dark surface meets the top edge of the MacBook screen/physical notch area. Refine the shape so the contact edges have small, intentional radii that visually match the physical notch instead of looking like a hard rectangular clash.

Keep the scope limited to the notch/status surface geometry and its tests or manual verification notes. Do not reintroduce liquid-glass styling or change unrelated board/card styling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The notch/status surface contact edges render with visible radii instead of hard square corners on notched displays.
- [ ] Non-notched and multi-display placement behavior remains intentional and does not gain surprising rounded artifacts.
- [ ] The darker current notch styling remains intact; no liquid-glass material or old translucent pill styling is reintroduced.
- [ ] Focused Swift tests cover the unit-testable shape/placement behavior, or the run log explains why visual/manual verification was required.
