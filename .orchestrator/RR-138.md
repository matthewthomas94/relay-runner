## Description

The notch status surface currently has hard, square-feeling corners where its shape meets the top edge of the screen. Refine the compact and expanded notch status shape so the top-edge contact corners read more like the physical macOS notch: attached to the screen edge, but with softened side transitions instead of visibly sharp edges.

Keep the change scoped to the notch/status surface geometry and rendering. Preserve existing placement, hover hit testing, trace labels, and board/activity behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The compact notch/status surface has softened corners where its left and right sides meet the top screen edge.
- [ ] The expanded trace/hover surface keeps the same softened contact-edge treatment without introducing detached gaps from the top edge.
- [ ] The radius treatment works on notched displays and external/non-notched display fallback placement.
- [ ] Hover and click targets remain stable after the shape change.
- [ ] Add focused regression coverage for the shape/geometry policy where practical, or document a manual visual verification scenario in the run log.
