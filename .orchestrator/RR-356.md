## Description

Normal app-started voice sessions can display the startup primer twice on screen. The bridge queues STARTUP_GREETING through _queue_tts_text in services/voice_bridge.py; tts_worker publishes its message_waiting preview. Independently AppState.surfaceSessionReadyIfNeeded calls StateMachine.showSessionReady, and OverlayController renders the same greeting. The user confirmed visual duplication, not repeated audio. The live September 19 session had a single greeting utterance accepted and queued at 14:34:36 local time, so downstream repeated playback is not established.

Choose the smallest coherent change giving the startup primer a single presentation owner. Preserve automatic speech when configured, manual playback and replay, normal response previews, and tutorial suppression. Do not silently suppress the first real messenger reply as a side effect of reusing the tutorial greeting flag. Consider greeting-first and readiness-first event ordering, recovery, and subsequent sessions. Avoid unrelated UI or voice changes.

The PM is separately handling microphone recovery. The previously running app executable differed from the installed bundle; relaunch restored screen capture and started fresh microphone capture. Do not change microphone capture, permissions, installed app, or user configuration in this worker.
